The Attic

Welcome to the Attic — my DIY music venue, lighting lab, and studio for me and my friends. What started as an empty upper floor for storage, transformed into a project like no other. From impromptu surprise parties, to a space for hosting friendsgiving, this space was built piece by piece with a lot of love (and Christmas lights).

Audio & Visual Setup

The space features a Pioneer XDJ-RX3, laser and light bar DMX lighting, reactive visuals and natural brick walls. It’s more than just a room — it’s a testbed for everything I’m learning in tech, music, and design.

Community & Vibe

Friends come here not just to party, but to connect, share, and build something together. Whether it's a relaxed board game night or a late-night-early-morning B2B2B2B session, the attic reflects the people inside it — creative, real, and just a little wild.
